Why now

Three things are true today

  • SqueezedDistributors say they earn ₹3.5–5 on every ₹100, and costs keep rising. So every rupee stuck in an unsettled claim hurts.All India Consumer Products Distributors Federation, June 2026
  • SlowDistributor claims take 45–90 days to settle, and about 1 in 3 are rejected, mostly over paperwork. So distributors cut the money from their next payment instead.Vendor-reported figures (Spirestock, Dec 2025)
  • New ruleFrom 1 Nov 2026, a brand gets GST back on an after-sale discount only with a proper GST credit note, and only once the distributor adjusts his tax credit.Finance Act 2026, CBIC Notification 03/2026

One example

₹1,180 goes missing. Here's how it ends.

  • Brand bills the distributor ₹11,800₹10,000 goods + ₹1,800 GST
  • Distributor pays ₹10,620. ₹1,180 is missing.He took off a 10% festival discount himself.
  • Every Rupee labels it: scheme claim. Owner: Priya. Due: Friday.Valid scheme, so it's approved.
  • Closed with a GST credit note of ₹1,180₹1,000 discount + ₹180 GST
  • Distributor adjusts his tax credit → brand recovers ₹180 GSTNext step on the roadmap: track each note as sent → accepted → adjusted.

Lose track of the credit note, and the brand pays that ₹180 of tax on money it never received. Multiply by thousands of claims.

How it works

Five steps, for every gap

  1. Payment arrivesThe bank statement is matched to open bills.
  2. Gap foundEvery short-payment is flagged automatically.
  3. Reason labelled8 business rules: scheme, damage, TDS and more.
  4. Owner and deadlineA second approver above ₹10,000. Unbudgeted schemes go to the regional sales head.
  5. ClosedGST credit note if fair, recovery if not. Logged with a reference.
