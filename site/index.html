    Where this would fail

    Three risks, and the design answer to each

    Distributors keep paying the old accountOld account stays open a quarter; Sales gets a weekly list of who still uses it.
    Scheme rules change mid-quarterNo scheme is valid until Sales enters its dates and budget. Anything else goes to a person.
    Sales overrides the credit flagThe flag never blocks an order; every override is logged by name and reviewed monthly.

    What it's worth

    How much cash is stuck in short-payments?

    –cash stuck at any time

    Billing × share × (days ÷ 30) = cash stuck; × cost of money = yearly cost. Defaults are examples, so try your own.
