Every rupee needs an owner.

In Indian distribution, a short payment is usually three things at once: tax withheld, a valid claim, and a dispute. Most teams treat it as one.

      Audit trail

        Next month Distributor A is due the same ₹8,000 scheme benefit. Instead of letting them deduct it, the claim is settled upfront.

        1. Scheme claim approved before payment. Credit note CN-0213 for ₹8,000 issued against the agreed scheme budget.
        2. Credit note reported to the IRP.
          Reported on day 2 of a 30-day window
        3. Statement sent. Invoice ₹10,00,000 − credit note ₹8,000 − expected TDS ₹1,000 = ₹9,91,000 due.
        4. Payment arrives on the virtual account: ₹9,91,000. Matched exactly. Unexplained gap: ₹0. No case opened.

        Credit-note deadlines built into the SLA

        IRP reportingWithin 30 days of issue, under the e-invoicing rule for turnover of ₹10 Cr and above (from 1 April 2025). The SLA alerts at day 20.
        GST credit note (Section 34)Issued no later than 30 November of the following year. A claim left in dispute past that date can lose its GST adjustment.

        Tax rules summarised for illustration; they change. Check current rules.

        Four layers

        1

        Identify

        Each distributor pays into its own virtual account number, so every receipt is tagged to the payer before anyone looks at it. An unreferenced partial payment settles the oldest open invoice first.

        2

        Split

        Statutory withholding is separated from commercial deductions. TDS u/s 194Q applies only when the buyer's previous-year turnover exceeds ₹10 crore and its purchases from the seller exceed ₹50 lakh in the year, at 0.1% of the amount above ₹50 lakh. The system tracks each distributor's running purchase total and expects a TDS line once the threshold is crossed. TDS is a credit to match against Form 26AS, not a loss. GST-TDS (2%) applies only to notified buyers such as government bodies.

        3

        Validate

        Every commercial deduction needs evidence. Evidenced, in-budget claims under a Finance-set tolerance are accepted automatically; people work only what is unexplained, unevidenced or abnormal.

        4

        Prevent

        Valid claims are settled upfront as credit notes, so the next payment arrives in full. Credit-note deadlines are part of the SLA.

        Reason code → evidence → validity rule

        Reason codeRequired evidenceValidity rule
        Scheme claimScheme circular or agreement, plus eligibilityValid only within that distributor's scheme budget
        Damage / shortageProof of delivery or damage reportFlagged if more than 2× the distributor's own trailing average
        Rate differencePrice list or contract in force on the invoice dateValid only against that price
        ReturnsGoods received noteValid for the quantity received
        No evidenceNoneDispute, with an owner and an SLA

        Auto-accepted

        Evidence present, within budget, under tolerance. Recorded with the rule that accepted it.

        Never automatic

        Unexplained gaps are never written off by a rule. They stay open with an owner until a person closes them.

        Kept from the original design: every case has a reason code and an owner, SLA routing, maker-checker above threshold, the ERP as system of record, and a full audit trail.

        What the research showed

        Treating TDS as a loss is a leading cause of reconciliation differences.

        Source: Terra Insight

        Source: HighRadius

        DecisionCost
        Control before speed. The first release fixes ownership and approvals.Less automation at launch; a faster process with no owner just moves the backlog.
        Strict matching at launch. Exact reference and amount only.A lower auto-match rate in exchange for zero wrong matches.

        No automatic write-off of unexplained gaps.Some small items stay open longer and need a person.
        Make the 90% boring. Auto-accept evidenced, in-budget claims.Needs a Finance-approved tolerance and clean evidence capture.
        Prevent before you reconcile. Settle valid claims as credit notes.Sales must agree scheme budgets upfront.

        How I'd measure it

        • Days deductions outstanding
        • Share of gaps auto-classified
        • Share of claims auto-accepted with evidence
        • Invalid deductions recovered
        • Credit notes reported within 30 days
        • Zero wrong write-offs

        One division in the pilot; targets set from the measured baseline.

        How I'd validate it

        • Interviews: AR executive, AR lead, Finance Controller, area sales manager, ERP owner
        • One month of anonymised receipts
        • Stop rule: if differences are rare or resolved within days, stop and redirect to wherever the finance team spends the most manual effort.
