How it works at the core: data in → logic → out
  • In: bank statement (one virtual account per distributor), open invoices, scheme budgets, claim evidence
  • Logic: match payer and oldest invoice · split the gap (TDS, scheme, damage, unexplained) · auto-accept only with evidence, within budget and tolerance · route the rest with an SLA
  • Out: a case queue with reason, owner and deadline · credit notes or recoveries with references · credit flag when disputes pile up
The 8 rules in plain English
  • BR-1 Identify the payer; a partial payment settles the oldest invoice first.
  • BR-2 Expect TDS where the law applies; match it to Form 26AS.
  • BR-3 Auto-accept only with evidence, within budget and tolerance.
  • BR-4 Never write off an unexplained gap automatically.
  • BR-5 Above ₹10,000, a second person approves.
  • BR-6 Settle valid claims as credit notes, reported on time.
  • BR-7 Flag the next order for credit review when disputes age or pile up.
  • BR-8 Unbudgeted scheme claims go to the regional sales head in 3 days.
