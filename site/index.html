        At a glance

        The problem

        A short payment is usually three things at once: , a valid claim, and a dispute. Most teams treat it as one, so the money sits unexplained while the distributor keeps ordering on credit.

        What I designed

        A workflow that identifies who paid, separates the tax, accepts claims backed by evidence, and gives every remaining rupee a reason, an owner and a deadline. Valid claims are then settled upfront as , so the next payment arrives in full.

        Audit trail

          Why this problem

          The case is built on Cello World Ltd, a consumer products maker selling through 565 dealers and distributors on 30–120 day credit. In FY26 its receivables grew faster than its revenue:

          +14.0%Receivables, ₹658 Cr → ₹750 Cr
          +8.8%Revenue, ₹2,136 Cr → ₹2,324 Cr
          ≈ ₹34 Crabove revenue pace (estimate)

          ₹658 Cr grown at 8.8% ≈ ₹716 Cr, against ₹750 Cr actual. An estimate, not a measured backlog; receivables also rise through credit expansion, channel mix or timing. It justifies discovery, not a diagnosis. In scope: receipts, deductions, their evidence, ownership and settlement. Out of scope: credit-limit policy, collections strategy and ERP replacement.

            Trade-offs I chose

            • Control before speed: ownership and approvals first, automation second.
            • Strict matching at launch: fewer auto-matches, zero wrong ones.
            • ERP stays the system of record: a workflow layer (proposed: a platform such as ServiceNow) posts only approved outcomes.
            • No automatic write-offs: small items may wait for a person.
            • Automate only the evidenced majority: needs a Finance-approved tolerance.
            • Prevent before you reconcile: Sales must agree scheme budgets upfront.

            How I'd measure it

            • Days deductions outstanding
            • Share of gaps auto-classified
            • Share of claims auto-accepted with evidence
            • Invalid deductions recovered
            • Credit notes reported within 30 days
            • Zero wrong write-offs

            How I'd validate it

            • Five 45-minute interviews: AR executive, AR lead, Finance Controller, area sales manager, ERP owner
            • One month of anonymised receipts
            • Stop rule: if gaps are rare or close within days, stop and redirect to wherever finance spends the most manual effort.
