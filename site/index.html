1 · The problem, today

Why short-payments pile up

Three things are true right now.

  • SqueezedDistributors say they earn ₹3.5–5 on every ₹100, and costs keep rising.All India Consumer Products Distributors Federation, June 2026
  • SlowTheir claims take 45–90 days to settle, about 1 in 3 rejected. So they cut the money from the next payment instead.Vendor-reported (Spirestock, Dec 2025)
  • New ruleFrom 1 Nov 2026, a brand recovers GST on an after-sale discount only with a proper GST credit note, once the distributor adjusts his tax credit.Finance Act 2026 · CBIC Notification 03/2026

2 · Who owns what

Every gap has exactly one owner at each step

The real failure today isn't the money. It's that nobody owns the gap.

FO

Finance ops (maker)Matches payments, checks the reason, recommends: credit note or recovery.Decides: the reason · Deadline: SLA clock per case

CK

CheckerApproves anything above ₹10,000. Can't approve their own case.Decides: approve or send back

SH

Regional sales headGets scheme claims with no approved budget: a promise made on the ground.Decides: charge to trade budget or recover · Deadline: 3 days

DS

DistributorReceives the credit note and adjusts his GST input credit.Needed for the brand to recover GST

3 · How it works at the core

Data in, rules, decisions out

Nothing is written off by a rule. Everything unclear goes to a person.

In

  • Bank statement (CSV), one virtual account per distributor
  • Open invoices
  • Approved scheme budgets and claim evidence
↓

Logic

  • Match each receipt to the payer and the oldest open invoice
  • Split the gap: TDS, scheme, damage, unexplained
  • Auto-accept only with evidence, within budget and tolerance
  • Route the rest to an owner, with an SLA
↓

Out

  • A case queue: every gap with reason, owner, deadline
  • Credit notes (fair) or recoveries (unfair), logged with a reference
  • Credit flag on the next order when disputes pile up

4 · The rules

8 rules, each one tested

Plain-language summary. The full wording is in the BRD.

  • BR-1Identify the payer by virtual account; a partial payment settles the oldest invoice first.
  • BR-2Expect TDS where the law applies, and match it to Form 26AS.
  • BR-3Auto-accept a claim only with evidence, within budget and tolerance. Otherwise, a person decides.
  • BR-4Never write off an unexplained gap automatically.
  • BR-5Above ₹10,000, a second person approves. No self-approval.
  • BR-6Settle valid claims as credit notes, reported on time.
  • BR-7When disputes age or pile up, flag the next order for credit review.
  • BR-8A scheme claim with no approved budget goes to the regional sales head within 3 days.
