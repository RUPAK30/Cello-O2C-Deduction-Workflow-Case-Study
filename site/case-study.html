Problem, trigger and proposed process

Why: the problem

One short payment mixes tax withheld, valid claims and disputes. Recorded as a single difference, it has no owner, and receivables outgrow revenue. Distributors short-pay partly because their claims take months to settle; settling valid claims fast removes the reason.

When: the trigger

A receipt posts below the invoice value.
Today weeks of email.
Target every rupee classified and owned the same day.

How: the proposed process
  1. Identify the payer (BR-1)
  2. Separate statutory TDS (BR-2)
  3. Validate claims against evidence (BR-3)
  4. Route the remainder to an owner with an SLA (BR-4 to BR-7)

The numbers behind it · Cello World's filings, FY25 to FY26

Why this problem

The case is built on Cello World Ltd, a consumer products maker selling through more than 4,000 distributors to over 1,50,000 retailers (FY25 investor presentation), mostly on credit. In FY26 its receivables grew faster than its revenue:

+14.0%Receivables, ₹658 Cr → ₹750 Cr
+8.8%Revenue, ₹2,136 Cr → ₹2,324 Cr
≈ ₹34 Crabove revenue pace (estimate)

₹658 Cr grown at 8.8% ≈ ₹716 Cr, against ₹750 Cr actual. An estimate, not a measured backlog; receivables also rise through credit expansion, channel mix or timing. It justifies discovery, not a diagnosis. In scope: receipts, deductions, their evidence, ownership and settlement. Out of scope: credit-limit policy, collections strategy and ERP replacement.

            Trade-offs, the one metric, and how I'd validate it

            Trade-offs I chose

            • Control before speed: ownership and approvals first, automation second.
            • Strict matching at launch: fewer auto-matches, zero wrong ones.
            • ERP stays the system of record: a workflow layer (proposed: a platform such as ServiceNow) posts only approved outcomes.
            • No automatic write-offs: small items may wait for a person.
            • Automate only the evidenced majority: needs a Finance-approved tolerance.
            • Prevent before you reconcile: Sales must agree scheme budgets upfront.

            How I'd measure it

            One number decides it: the share of short-paid rupees with a class and an owner on the day the payment arrives. Target: all of them.

            It should move one business outcome: receivables growing faster than revenue (₹658 Cr → ₹750 Cr against +8.8% revenue).

            Guardrails, not goals: zero wrong write-offs; credit notes reported within 30 days.

            How I'd validate it

            • Five 45-minute interviews: AR executive, AR lead, Finance Controller, area sales manager, ERP owner
            • One month of anonymised receipts
            • Stop rule: if gaps are rare or close within days, stop and redirect to wherever finance spends the most manual effort.

            Where this would fail · three risks and the design answer to each

            Where this would fail

            Distributors keep paying the old account

            Virtual accounts only identify the payer if they are used. Design answer: the old account stays open for a quarter with receipts matched by the oldest-invoice rule, and Sales gets a weekly list of distributors still using it.

            Scheme rules change mid-quarter

            Auto-acceptance is only as good as the budget it checks. Design answer: no scheme is valid until Sales enters it with dates and a budget; claims against an unentered scheme go to a person, never through.

            Sales overrides the credit flag

            A flag nobody respects is noise. Design answer: the flag never blocks an order, but every override is logged with a name, and overrides per area appear in the monthly review.

            How this evolved · v1 assignment → v2 research → v3 executable spec

            How this evolved

            1. v1 · The assignment. A deduction workflow: a case with a reason code and owner, SLA routing, maker-checker, ERP as system of record.
            2. v2 · The research. TDS treated as a loss, 90–95% of deduction value usually valid, missed credit-note windows: answered by four layers (Identify, Split, Validate, Prevent).
            3. v3 · An executable specification. The page had become a long read. The rules now run as a desk: the ten UAT cases execute against it and report pass or fail, and every action is traced to its rule, story and test. The full specification sits behind one tap.
