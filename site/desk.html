New to finance? The 6 words you need
Short-paid
The distributor paid less than the bill said.
TDS
Tax the buyer must keep back and pay to the government. Not a loss, just matched to a tax form.
Scheme claim
A discount the brand promised, like a festival offer, that the distributor took off the payment.
Credit note
The document that officially reduces a bill, so the books and GST match what was paid.
Maker-checker
One person proposes, a second approves. Nobody approves their own big decision.
Audit trail
A log of who did what and when, so anyone can check it later.
