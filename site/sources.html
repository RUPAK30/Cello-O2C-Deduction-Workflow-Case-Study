What Every Rupee does not claim

  • That it describes Cello World's actual processes. Internal processes are hypotheses built from public information.
  • That any brand uses it, or that it has reduced anyone's receivables.
  • That it connects to an ERP or bank, or files anything with GST. It's a working prototype with sample data.
  • That lender mode reflects any lender's actual policy. Penal amount, re-presentation limit, appropriation order and waiver threshold are illustrative settings.
  • That the calculator is a measurement. It's arithmetic on numbers you type in.
