1. Purpose and background

When a distributor pays less than the invoice, the gap usually mixes tax withheld, valid commercial claims and genuine disputes. Treated as one unexplained amount, it waits in email until month-end, delays cash application and hides real overdue balances while the distributor keeps ordering on credit.

Signal: in FY26 Cello's trade receivables rose 14.0% (₹658 Cr → ₹750 Cr) against revenue growth of 8.8% (₹2,136 Cr → ₹2,324 Cr), roughly ₹34 Cr above revenue pace. This is an estimate, not a measured backlog; receivables also move with credit policy, channel mix and timing. It justifies discovery, not a diagnosis.

2. Scope

In scope: distributor receipts, short payments and deductions; their identification, classification, evidence, ownership, approval and settlement as credit notes; a credit-review flag.

Out of scope: credit-limit policy, collections strategy, ERP replacement and supplier-side (procure-to-pay) matching.

4. Current state (hypothesised) and pain points

  1. P1 No remittance detail: payments can't be tied to specific invoices.
  2. P2 Unstructured reasons: the cause of a difference lives in emails, not in a reportable field.
  3. P3 Unclear ownership: Finance and Sales each assume the other is chasing.
  4. P4 Weak approval trail: deductions are accepted over email; audit evidence is scattered.
  5. P5 Late visibility: ageing exceptions surface only at month-end.

7. Functional requirements (user stories)

    8. Data requirements

    Distributor ID, virtual account number, invoice number and amount, receipt reference and amount received, gap, expected TDS and Form 26AS match status, reason code, evidence reference, owner, SLA due date, status, maker, checker, credit note number, IRP reporting date, and a timestamp for every status change.

    9. Controls and non-functional requirements

    • Maker-checker above the Finance-set threshold; self-approval blocked and logged.
    • No automatic write-off of unexplained gaps; tolerances set and changed only by Finance, with the change logged.
    • Full audit trail per case; reassignment keeps history.
    • Role-based access; daily reconciliation of case totals to the ERP.
    • The ERP remains the system of record; the workflow layer (proposed: a platform such as ServiceNow) posts only approved outcomes. Integration method to confirm with IT.

    11. Success metrics and pilot

    Days deductions outstanding · share of gaps auto-classified · share of claims auto-accepted with evidence · invalid deductions recovered · credit notes reported within 30 days · zero wrong write-offs.

    Pilot: one division, one month of historical receipts run offline (subject to security approval), then four to six weeks live. Targets are set from the measured baseline. Go / no-go: continue only if accuracy holds, resolution time falls against the baseline, and AR and Sales use the queue.

    12. Risks and dependencies

    • Remittance and evidence data quality; distributor adoption of virtual accounts and claim evidence.
    • ERP integration effort and platform licensing.
    • Sales agreeing scheme budgets upfront and owning dispute cases.
    • Tax rules changing; thresholds and deadlines must be configurable, not hard-coded.
